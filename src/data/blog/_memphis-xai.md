I'm fortunate enough to have traveled to Memphis, Tennessee quite a few times with work. It's a city with problems, but I've always enjoyed the times I've spent there. Everyone I know from there is very proud to call it home.

Southaven, Mississipi is in the next state down but also, weirdly, a suburb of Memphis, such is the proximity of both to the Tennessee / Mississipi state line.

This is where Elon Musk's new [xAI](https://x.ai/) data centre is going.
