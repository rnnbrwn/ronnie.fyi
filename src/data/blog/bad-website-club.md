Starting on Friday 24 April, and with daily lessons at 3PM UTC, the folks at [badwebsiteclub.com](https://badwebsite.club) are offering to teach you how to build a website.

> Bad Website Club has been running free and online developer education programs since 2020. It's run by a small volunteer team ([Jessica Rose](https://jessica.tech/), [Carmen Huidobro](https://carmenh.dev/), and [Eda Eren](https://edaeren.com/) – also thank you to wonderful [Kiri](https://kirionearth.com/) who made all the art!).

Seems like a cool way to get involved in a growing community and working through the hundreds of lessons on [Free Code Camp](https://www.freecodecamp.org/).
