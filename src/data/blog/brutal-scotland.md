[Simon Phipps](https://www.simonphipps.co.uk/)' [Brutal Scotland](https://www.duckworthbooks.co.uk/book/brutal-scotland/) is another in his series looking at brutalist architecture in the UK. See also Brutal London, Brutal North, Brutal Wales etc. Man likes his concrete.

The work featured is currently being exhibited **for free** in Glasgow's [Street Level Photoworks](https://www.streetlevelphotoworks.org/event/simon-phipps-brutal-scotland) gallery.

If that's your thing, you should also check out [Nebo Peklo's work](https://www.nebo-peklo.co.uk/). I have this print on my wall at home.
